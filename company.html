About the company

The Northstar platform

Northstar provides a member dashboard for account details, membership information, product listings, commission estimates, and administrator-confirmed wallet credits. Members can view information associated with their own account, while authorized administrators manage account records and platform entries.

Commission values are estimates, not guaranteed returns. Wallet credits are entered by an administrator after confirming payment outside the app; the platform does not process payments, purchases, bidding, transfers, or withdrawals.
